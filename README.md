## 💡 The Core Problem: A Predatory $65 Billion Industry
Over 12 million Mexican expatriates in the United States send **$65+ billion annually** back home to sustain their families.
- **Traditional Legacy Middlemen (Western Union, MoneyGram, traditional wire transfers):**
  - Charge **4% to 8% in hidden FX markup and front-end transaction fees**.
  - Impose painful **24 to 72-hour settlement delays**.
  - Require vulnerable physical retail collection points (stores/pharmacies) subject to theft, extortion, and long lines.
  - Siphon over **$3.5 billion in predatory friction fees** out of the hands of working-class families every year.

---

## ⚡ The Solana Solution: RemitSol
RemitSol eliminates predatory intermediaries by transitioning cross-border transfers into **instant, programmable USDC streaming on Solana**:
1. **Sub-Second Finality:** 400ms settlement times at ~$0.00025 per transfer.
2. **Streaming Allowances:** Workers can deposit funds once and stream weekly or daily allowances to relatives' non-custodial wallets.
3. **Emergency Revoke:** Senders retain cryptographic cancellation rights if funds are uncollected.
4. **Instant SPEI Off-Ramp Integration:** Direct liquidity connection to Mexican banking rails (SPEI via Bitso/STP) for instant Mexican Peso (MXN) local spendability.
